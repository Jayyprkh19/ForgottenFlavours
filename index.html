Preserving Earth's Rare and Endangered Species

What is Forgotten Flavors?

"Forgotten Flavors is a student showcase project I created for my Web Technology subject. Inspired by resources like worldwildlife.org, this website highlights the unique and endangered spices and animals that are often overlooked. Many of these are threatened by climate change, deforestation, and overharvesting. Through this site, I aim to raise awareness and share the beauty of these forgotten natural treasures.
